Quiz Me Baby One More Time is a trivia app for iPhone and iPad. Pick categories, difficulty, and question type, then play a round from [Open Trivia DB](https://opentdb.com). A quokka mascot cheers you on as you go.

This page is the support and privacy page for the App Store listing.

{% include screenshot-gallery.html %}

## Support

Email [rabbidyak@gmail.com](mailto:rabbidyak@gmail.com) with the app name **Quiz Me Baby One More Time** in the subject. Please include your device type and iOS version if you are reporting a problem.

## What the app does

- Quiz tab to exclude categories, pick difficulty, choose multiple choice or true/false, and set how many questions (up to 50)
- Tap **Hit me** to start a round
- After every five answers, the quokka checks in with a reaction
- Results screen with your score and a button to play another round
- Live questions from Open Trivia DB, or a copy you download onto this device
- A full download is about 5,300 questions, uses about 3 MB, and takes about nine minutes because Open Trivia DB allows one request every five seconds. Keep the app open while it runs. You can stop and resume, update later, or delete the local copy
- Appearance that follows the system look, or locks light or dark mode
- Optional OpenDyslexic typeface in Settings

## Important notice

Questions come from Open Trivia Database and can include adult themes. The live catalog is unfiltered, so some questions are aimed at older players (roughly 13+). A downloaded copy uses the same source and license.

## Privacy

The full legal notice is the [Privacy Policy](privacy/). A short summary of how this app uses data is below.

Quiz Me Baby One More Time does not create an account with us, show ads, or sell your information.

| Data | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Settings (appearance, OpenDyslexic, question source) | Stored on your device |
| Quiz score | Kept in memory for the current round on your device |
| Local question library | Optional. A downloaded copy stays on this device until you delete it |
| Live trivia requests | The app may download categories and questions from Open Trivia DB over HTTPS. Those requests are not used to identify you |

We do not collect analytics, crash reports, or advertising IDs.
