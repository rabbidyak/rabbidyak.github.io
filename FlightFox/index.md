Fini's Flight Fox shows how reliable a route has been before you book. It is for iPhone. It does not sell tickets.

This page is the support and privacy page for the App Store listing.

{% include screenshot-gallery.html %}

## Support

Email [rabbidyak@gmail.com](mailto:rabbidyak@gmail.com) with the app name **Fini's Flight Fox** in the subject. Please include your device type and iOS version if you are reporting a problem.

## What the app does

- Look up a city or an airport code
- See the nonstop, and one- or two-stop paths whose typical same-day layover meets the minimum you set
- Pick a travel date. Scores follow that weekday. Season uses the month of the trip, the month before it, and the month after it. All months uses the whole period
- Open an airline, an airport, or one route for the on-time rate, cancellations, diversions, median delay, the day of the week, and reported delay minutes
- Keep a home airport on the first screen
- Appearance that follows the system look, or locks light or dark mode
- Prices open on Google Flights for the date you picked

Version 1.0 includes US reporting-carrier flights from September 2025 through August 2026: 7,049,869 scheduled flights. A flight is on time when it arrives less than 15 minutes after the scheduled gate time. Trip reliability divides those on-time arrivals by every scheduled flight, so a cancellation or a diversion counts as a miss.

## Important notice

A score is history from public reports. It is not a forecast for that calendar day, and it is not a published itinerary or an airline connection rule.

## Privacy

The full legal notice is the [Privacy Policy](privacy/). A short summary of how this app uses data is below.

Fini's Flight Fox does not create an account, show ads, or sell your information.

| Data | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Settings (appearance, home airport, travel date, season, minimum layover) | Stored on your device |
| Flight figures | Included in the app. They are not sent anywhere |
| Prices | Opens one Google Flights search in Safari. Google's policy applies on that page |
| Source tables | Opens the US Department of Transportation on-time page in Safari |

We do not collect analytics, crash reports, or advertising IDs. Fini's Flight Fox does not receive anything you do on those Safari pages.

## Sources

Figures come from public reports and are not mixed together into one score.

- US Department of Transportation, Bureau of Transportation Statistics, Reporting Carrier On-Time Performance, September 2025 through August 2026
- T-100 International Segment file, for departures performed on flights with one end outside the United States. That count has no on-time rate
- UK Civil Aviation Authority, Australia's Bureau of Infrastructure and Transport Research Economics, and Eurocontrol, shown beside the US score
- [OpenFlights](https://openflights.org) route map where no agency figure exists. That map has no year
- [Google Flights](https://www.google.com/travel/flights) for prices only
